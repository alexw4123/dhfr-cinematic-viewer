    Models, methods & interpretation+

    The source files

    Each complex contains 3,153 atoms and 3,195 explicit bonds: 3,013 human DHFR atoms, 74 modeled NADPH atoms, and 66 candidate atoms. Display PDBs add connectivity from matched source PSF files. Atom identities and coordinates are unchanged; no inferred bonds or candidate–protein covalent bonds are added.

    Atomic is the default. Secondary structure is NGL’s coordinate-based assignment of the protein, with NADPH and the candidate kept atomic. Highlighting does not alter geometry. Isolation is optional and explicit; camera controls change the view only.

    Pocket context comprises whole protein residues with a heavy atom within 6 Å of a candidate heavy atom, plus NADPH and the candidate. It is a display selection, not a contact-energy or hydrogen-bond calculation.

    Before simulation or biological claims

    These static, unsolvated 4M6K-derived preparations are unrelaxed. The report identifies unresolved candidate overlaps and the need to review protonation and force-field accuracy. Atom matching and parameter coverage are preparation checks, not evidence that the poses bind.

    No current production DHFR trajectory, candidate inhibition assay, cellular uptake, selectivity, or safety result is shown here. Archived 1U72 technical pilots are separate from these 4M6K models.

    The Smith-Carpenter Lab’s work covers molecular design and planned synthesis. Phelan Lab collaboration concerns planned inhibition and cellular testing after suitable material is available.
